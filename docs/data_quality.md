# Data quality control

The corpus is synthetic (sentences generated by coding-agent CLIs, one intended
sense each) and admitted only when the rule engine agreed with the intended
label. That makes the rule engine useless as a self-check — it *assigned* the
labels. So QC uses **independent** signals and reviews only where they disagree,
which is far cheaper than reading 87k lines and is where real errors concentrate.

## Method

1. **Multi-method prediction.** Every corpus line is re-labelled by three signals
   independent of the rule engine: spaCy POS → sense (`pt_core_news_lg`), the
   Naive-Bayes model, and the perceptron model.
2. **Disagreement flagging.** A line is flagged when an independent signal
   contradicts the stored label. spaCy alone flagged 36% — almost all spaCy's own
   POS mistakes on minority (verb) readings, since the corpus is balanced 50/50
   while spaCy is noun-biased. The precise signal is **both trained models
   disagreeing** (170 lines corpus-wide).
3. **Stratified adjudication.** A native-Portuguese LLM agent judged each
   sentence's sense *blind to the stored label*: all 170 model-disagreement lines
   plus random samples of the spaCy-only bucket (250) and the all-agree bucket
   (150) as controls.

## Findings (confident disagreements)

| stratum | n | errors | rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| both models disagree | 170 | 51 | **30.0%** |
| spaCy-only disagrees | 250 | 4 | 1.6% |
| all methods agree | 150 | 2 | 1.3% |

The model-disagreement signal is ~30% precision for real label errors versus a
~1.3% baseline — a 23× enrichment. Errors were **systematic**, not random:
`forma: shape→mould` was 39 of 51 (the generator labelled baking-tin / casting
*fôrma* sentences, closed-o `ˈfoɾmɐ`, as the open-o "shape" reading), plus a
thinner stream of `sede: seat→thirst` and `molho: bundle→sauce`.

## Actions

- 57 verified relabels applied; 1 defective sentence (target word used in a sense
  outside the roster) dropped.
- **Residual check:** a fresh sample of 120 shape-labelled `forma` lines *not* in
  the original sample was re-adjudicated — 1 contamination (0.8%), confirming the
  model-disagreement stratum had already isolated the systematic errors.

## Estimated corpus quality

Bulk (all-methods-agree, ~64% of lines) ≈ 98.7% correct; spaCy-only-flagged
bucket ≈ 98.4%; the model-flagged bucket is now cleaned. Overall label accuracy
≈ **98.5%**, with the dominant systematic error class removed.

Reproduce: `qc_phase1.py` (flagging) is in the repo scratch; the per-stratum
sampling and agent adjudication are described above and re-runnable on any corpus
revision.
