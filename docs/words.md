# Portuguese Heterophonic Homographs — Word Reference

Each word below is spelled identically across its meanings but pronounced differently.
The diacritic convention used in this project:

| Diacritic | Vowel | Example |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| acute (ó/é/á) | open vowel /ɔ/, /ɛ/, /a/ | pára (stops) |
| circumflex (ô/ê) | closed vowel /o/, /e/ | pêlo (fur) |
| no diacritic | default / ADP reading | para (to), pelo (by) |

---

## acordo

**Canonical form:** acordo

| POS | IPA | Diacritized | Meaning | Example |
|-----|-----|-------------|---------|---------|
| NOUN | ˈkoɾdu | acôrdo | agreement, deal | *O acôrdo de paz foi assinado.* |
| VERB | ˈkɔɾdu | acórdo | I wake up (1st person of *acordar*) | *Acórdo sempre cedo nos dias úteis.* |

**For non-native speakers:** the NOUN "acordo" (closed-o) is what you reach after a negotiation — "agreement". The VERB "acórdo" (open-o) is "I wake up" (from the verb *acordar*). The stressed vowel shifts from closed /o/ to open /ɔ/.

**Disambiguation:** "acordo" after a determiner (o acordo, um acordo) is nearly always NOUN. Sentence-initial or after a pronoun (eu acordo, sempre acordo) is VERB.

## corte

**Canonical form:** corte

## forma

**Canonical form:** forma

| POS | IPA | Diacritized | Meaning | Example |
|-----|-----|-------------|---------|---------|
| NOUN | ˈfoɾmɐ | fôrma | baking mold, tin, pan | *A fôrma do bolo de mel é redonda.* |
| NOUN | ˈfɔɾmɐ | fórma | manner, way; geometric shape | *Desta fórma é mais fácil.* / *Uma fórma geométrica.* |
| VERB | ˈfɔɾmɐ | fórma | (he/she) forms, shapes (3rd person of *formar*) | *O sol fórma colunas de ar quente.* |

**⚠ Important:** Most NOUN uses of "forma" — including "geometric shape" and "manner/way" — are pronounced with **open-o (ˈfɔɾmɐ)**, the same as the VERB reading. Only the **baking mold** meaning takes closed-o (ˈfoɾmɐ). The compound **pão-de-forma** (sandwich loaf) is always closed-o. In practice, ~95% of occurrences of "forma" use open-o.

**For non-native speakers:** think of "desta forma" (in this way), "forma geométrica" (geometric shape), "forma de vida" (way of life) — all open-o. The baking mold is the special case requiring closed-o.

## gosto

**Canonical form:** gosto

| POS | IPA | Diacritized | Meaning | Example |
|-----|-----|-------------|---------|---------|
| NOUN | ˈgoʃtu | gôsto | taste, flavour; preference, liking | *Tem um gôsto refinado para música.* |
| NOUN | ˈgɔʃtu | gósto | social-media "like" (21st c. slang) | *Dei um gósto à publicação dela.* |
| VERB | ˈgɔʃtu | gósto | I like (1st person of *gostar*) | *Gósto muito de música clássica.* |

**⚠ New meaning (social media):** In contemporary Portuguese social media, "dar um gosto" (to give a like) uses **open-o (ˈgɔʃtu)** — the same as the VERB. This is a recent calque from Facebook/Instagram "like". The traditional NOUN "gôsto" (taste, flavor) retains closed-o.

**For non-native speakers:** "gôsto" (closed-o) = the noun "taste" — "tem bom gosto" = has good taste. "Gósto" (open-o) = "I like" (verb), and in modern usage also a social-media like/reaction.

## para

**Canonical form:** para

| POS | IPA | Diacritized | Meaning | Example |
|-----|-----|-------------|---------|---------|
| ADP | ˈpɐɾɐ | para | to, for, towards (destination/purpose) | *Vou para casa.* |
| VERB | ˈpaɾɐ | pára | stops, halts (3rd person of *parar*) | *O autocarro pára aqui.* |

**For non-native speakers:** "para" (ADP, unstressed) is one of the most common Portuguese prepositions — direction ("vou para o trabalho"), purpose ("para comer"), recipient ("é para ti"). "Pára" (VERB, stressed open-a) is "stops/halts". Written with acute accent before AO1990; today both are written "para".

**Hard cases:** "pára a medicação" (stops the medication) looks identical locally to "para a casa" (to the house). A sequence model is needed to distinguish these reliably.

## pelo

**Canonical form:** pelo

| POS | IPA | Diacritized | Meaning | Example |
|-----|-----|-------------|---------|---------|
| ADP | ˈpɨlu | pelo | by, through, along (contraction of *por + o*) | *Passou pelo parque.* |
| NOUN | ˈpelu | pêlo | body hair, fur, coat (of an animal) | *O gato perdeu muito pêlo.* |
| VERB | ˈpɛlu | pélo | I peel / I skin (1st person of *pelar*) | *Pélo as batatas antes de cozinhar.* |

**For non-native speakers:** "pelo" (ADP) = "by/through" — it contracts the preposition *por* with the article *o*. "Pêlo" (NOUN, closed-e) = fur, body hair. "Pélo" (VERB, open-e) = "I peel/skin". The ADP reading is overwhelmingly the most common.

## posto

**Canonical form:** posto

| POS | IPA | Diacritized | Meaning | Example |
|-----|-----|-------------|---------|---------|
| NOUN | ˈpoʃtu | pôsto | post, position, station; petrol station | *O pôsto de saúde fica ao fundo da rua.* |
| VERB | ˈpɔʃtu | pósto | I post/upload (1st person of *postar*) | *Pósto fotos de viagem nas redes sociais.* |

**⚠ Passive participle note:** The past participle of *pôr* (to put/place) is also "posto" — but it is pronounced with **closed-o (ˈpoʃtu)**, same as the NOUN. "O livro foi posto na prateleira" = closed-o, even though it is grammatically a verbal form. Our scorer returns NOUN IPA for passive constructions to reflect this.

**For non-native speakers:** "pôsto" (NOUN) covers military/police posts, job positions, and petrol stations. "Pósto" (VERB, open-o) is the social-media / logistics sense — "I post/upload/deploy". "Foi posto" (was placed/put) = closed-o, like the NOUN.

## sobre

**Canonical form:** sobre

| POS | IPA | Diacritized | Meaning | Example |
|-----|-----|-------------|---------|---------|
| ADP | ˈsobɾɨ | sobre | about, on, over (preposition) | *Falaram sobre o projecto.* |
| NOUN | ˈsobɾɨ | sôbre | envelope (letter envelope) | *Colocou a carta no sôbre azul.* |
| VERB | ˈsɔbɾɨ | sóbre | is left over (3rd person of *sobrar*) | *Sóbre sempre pão ao jantar.* |

**⚠ ADP and NOUN share the same IPA** (ˈsobɾɨ, closed-o). The disambiguation task for "sobre" is ADP/NOUN vs VERB. The VERB "sobrar" (to be left over, to be in excess) is less common and often sentence-initial.

**For non-native speakers:** "sobre" as a preposition = "about/on/over" (very common). "Sôbre" as a noun = envelope (the paper sleeve for a letter). "Sóbre" as a verb = "there is (something) left over" — "sóbre comida" = "there's food left over".

## tola

**Canonical form:** tola

| POS | IPA | Diacritized | Meaning | Example |
|-----|-----|-------------|---------|---------|
| ADJ | ˈtɔlɐ | tóla | silly, foolish (feminine adjective) | *Que ideia tóla!* |
| NOUN | ˈtolɐ | tôla | the name "Tola" (female given name); a foolish person (substantivised) | *A Tôla lá da aldeia era simpática.* |

**For non-native speakers:** "tóla" (ADJ, open-o) describes something or someone as silly/foolish — "que tóla, esqueceu o guarda-chuva!" = "how silly, she forgot her umbrella!". "Tôla" (NOUN, closed-o) is both a female given name and the substantivised form ("a tola" = the foolish one). Context almost always resolves this: a name is capitalised and takes a definite article; an exclamative use without an object is ADJ.
